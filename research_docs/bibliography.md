- [Generative AI Profile (NIST.AI.600-1)](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ai/NIST.AI.600-1.pdf) — harmful bias, violent content, privacy, information integrity. [doi](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.AI.600-1)
- [Identifying and Managing Bias in AI (NIST.SP.1270)](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/SpecialPublications/NIST.SP.1270.pdf) — demographic/bias axes. [doi](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.SP.1270)
